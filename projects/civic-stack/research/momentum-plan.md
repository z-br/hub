# Momentum Plan: The Portland Civic Stack (July 18, 2026)

> Output of a 7-agent panel: five lenses (systems/flywheel, community organizer, local-media strategist, solo-builder sustainability, civic power/politics) → devil's-advocate stress test → synthesis with the critique's corrections binding.

*Three corrections govern everything below: (1) re-anchor to the actual July 2026 calendar — the April vacancy-tax debate already happened and the "naming window" is dropped as a clock; (2) one sequence rule resolves all conflicts: **consent → publicity → product**; (3) trust infrastructure and a measured baseline come before anything public, inside a hard 3 hr/week cap.*

## 1. How it fits together

Three assets, one loop, one scarce resource.

**Rose Light is the eyes.** It reads 22 Portland news sources around the clock and sorts what matters, for pennies, without GZ touching it. **The research corpus is the memory** — checked facts about the bridge, the vacancies, and who holds which role. **Better Burnside is the hands** — the campaign that turns those facts into things people can act on.

The loop: Rose Light's automation keeps the Burnside site's news section fresh with zero ongoing effort, so a one-person project looks staffed. A fresh site plus a carefully verified vacancy dataset earn GZ a seat at the table with the people who already champion the corridor — Hashagen, Stickler, Sweat, O'Meara. Those champions, not GZ, become the coalition's public face. Coalition activity then produces signups and stories that flow back into Rose Light, which makes the next conversation easier.

The Recovery Tracker is deliberately parked: a research document plus one static section until a councilor, reporter, or partner asks for more. Building it now would be the fun move and the wrong one.

What compounds: the subscriber list (with source tags), the article archive under each topic, and relationships. What decays: any published status nobody re-verifies.

The governing constraint: ~3 hours a week. The machines exist so those hours go almost entirely to the one thing machines cannot do — sitting across from people and earning their consent.

## 2. The load-bearing connections (ranked)

1. **Rose Light tag layer → Burnside corridor news + bridge-funding watcher.** Self-refreshing campaign site at near-zero cost; compounds structurally as the archive accretes. Justified by the corridor alone.
2. **Hardened vacancy inventory → champion and stadium-end conversations → standing.** GZ's only original data; his admission ticket. Compounds through *co-authorship* (asking Goose Hollow to correct their blocks converts audience into collaborators). Unhardened, it's a liability.
3. **Burnside signup → Rose Light Subscriber, `source="betterburnside"`.** The one connection that makes the flywheel measurable.
4. **Bridge-funding advocacy as a gift to Multnomah County.** The rare flow where GZ offers an institution something it wants ($740M federal ask) before asking for anything.

Conflicts resolved: consent-first beats press-first (no pitch without a champion's quote). Sustainability beats editorial on the tracker (deferred, demand-activated). The 13-vector board comes OFF the Burnside site (corridor vector + bridge watcher only). Digest stays 30 minutes (Claude drafts, GZ edits).

## 4. What breaks it
1. **Launching over the champions' heads** — unrecoverable. Mitigation: consent → publicity → product; drafts only; the name is theirs.
2. **One wrong "vacant" claim** — the likely wronged parties are exactly who must be recruited. Mitigation: hardening, as-of dates, owner contact, correction channel, field-verified-only rule.
3. **Hours creep** — the lenses' union was 10-15 hrs/wk. Mitigation: 3-hr cap, displacement rule, parked tracker, quarterly delegated walks.
4. **Stale facts under a "verified" brand.** Mitigation: calendar re-anchor, monthly verification PR, EQRB event-trigger.
5. **The code-at-night default** — building alone is the only move requiring no other person, and the one the system needs least. Mitigation: builds are bounded weekends; the weekly block is talk-only; success measured by what champions do.
