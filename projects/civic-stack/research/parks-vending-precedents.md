# Parks Vending + Beer Garden Precedents (activation & revenue)

## Portland status (premise update!)
- **PP&R Mobile Vending Program launched Oct 2025**: $5/day most parks ($10-40 premium: Washington Pk, Hoyt, Pittock), Parking Kitty app, 35+ parks, no contracts. Contact: Danny Dunn, ParksCommercialUse@portlandoregon.gov.
- **Gaps = next asks**: parking areas only (not in-park); NO seating/equipment/signs allowed; alcohol not included. So: vending NEAR parks, not IN parks.
- Beer gardens already legal AS EVENTS: PP&R special-use permit + OLCC temporary license (every summer festival does it). A rotating program needs policy will, not law change.

## Precedents
- **Philadelphia Parks on Tap** (THE model): Parks&Rec + private operator (FCM Hospitality), traveling beer garden rotating ~14+ parks/summer since 2016, food + lawn games + family programming, revenue share back to parks ("the beer garden that gives back"). 2026 season running. A decade of answers to liability/fencing/rotation objections. parksontap.com
- **NYC**: concession licensing = tens of $M/yr to Parks; Shake Shack began as Madison Sq Park kiosk; Bryant Park ~fully concession/event funded.
- **Minneapolis**: Sea Salt Eatery inside Minnehaha Falls park — beer-serving park concession, beloved.
- **Chicago Riverwalk**: city-owned wine-bar concessions turned dead corridor into evening destination.
- **Seattle (our research)**: Waterfront Park season one = 68 merchants + 11 food vendors, $2.5M vendor revenue — vending as core conservancy programming.
- **Munich Englischer Garten**: the cultural proof alcohol + families coexist in parks.

## Portland asks (escalating)
1. Let permitted vendors set up IN parks with seating (close the parking-lot gap).
2. Pilot a rotating "Parks on Tap PDX" summer garden — 3 parks, operator partnership, revenue share. Natural route: the Burnside parks string (Couch Park / North Park Blocks / stadium-adjacent on match days). Hashagen (Icicle Tricycles) = obvious vendor-side ally.
3. Formalize concessions as a PP&R revenue line (NYC model) — relevant to parks budget crisis.

Safety frame: more people = safer (Couch Park Coffee Club proves it at 11am; a beer garden is the 6pm version). Street Roots covered the vending program with the same activation framing.
