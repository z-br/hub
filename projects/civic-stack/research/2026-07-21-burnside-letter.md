- **Advocacy: Better Block PDX, Strong Towns PDX, BikeLoud, Oregon Walks, Families for Safe Streets, 1000 Friends of Oregon**

## The forcing event (this is the real clock now, not the bridge)
PBOT announced a **22-block W Burnside pavement resurfacing**; **Phase 1 ($2.4M, 15th–20th Ave — the stadium blocks/vacancy-cluster segment) is ALREADY UNDERWAY**, rebuilding the existing lane configuration with only ~1 month of public notice. The letter asks PBOT to pause and take community input; wants redesign elements (protected bike lanes, transit access, safer crossings, wider sidewalks), warning repaving-as-is "threatens to lock Burnside in as a High Crash Corridor" for ~two decades. Safety data cited: 4 fatalities on W Burnside in the last decade.

PBOT response (comms dir Hannah Schafer): agrees the street "has many needs beyond the pavement condition"; pavement repair was a long-standing community request; **design for later phases remains open to community input** ← the opening.
