# The pool, the seam, and the cluster — Alder/Harvey Milk momentum vs the Burnside vacancy cluster

GZ's observation (2026-07-21): "there actually seems to be a lot of momentum on SW Alder —
Darcelle plaza, Flock, West End, the midtown beer garden." Verified below. The precise story:
the momentum is a **pool along SW Alder + Harvey Milk between 5th and 9th**, 3–6 short blocks
south of W Burnside, at the foot of the West End — not the West End interior, and not Burnside.

## The four verified data points

| Site | Where | Opened | Off Burnside | Public role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Midtown Beer Garden | 431 SW Harvey Milk @ 5th | Aug 20, 2023 | 3 blocks (~4 min) | Legacy pod (city's oldest, est. early 1980s), renovated; 20+ carts, 300 covered seats, Fracture Brewing bar, stage |
| Flock Food Hall | 917 SW Alder @ 9th (Block 216 / Ritz-Carlton; the old Alder cart block) | Feb 2, 2025 | 6 blocks (~7 min) | **$3M Prosper Portland loan**; 8,000 SF, 9 vendors |
| Darcelle XV Plaza | 800 SW Harvey Milk @ Park (ex-O'Bryant Square) | Jun 18, 2026 (ribbon; fully open Jun 25) | 3 blocks (~4 min) | Portland Parks capital project; stage + event space |
| James Beard Public Market | SW 6th & Alder (Selling Building) | Phased, fall 2026 | 4 blocks (~5 min) | Prosper predevelopment grant; ~40,000 SF, 40 stalls |

Corrections vs the casual framing: Flock is NOT in the West End proper (it's Block 216, 9th &
Alder — the West End's SE corner); Midtown Beer Garden is at **5th**, not the West End at all.
The coherent geography is the **midtown Alder/Harvey Milk spine, 5th→9th**, pooling toward the
Park Blocks. Discarded search noise: "Henny's West End" = Portland **Maine**. Unverified lead:
Mako Matcha Mill, "West End" (one mention, Bridgetown Bites 2026 openings list).

Timing flag: Prosper's own page said JBPM "late 2025"; our July 2026 site data says phased
opening fall 2026. Re-verify JBPM's current date before citing publicly.

## Why it matters for Better Burnside

1. **Evidence, not a new vector.** Demand is proven 3–6 blocks from the corridor — Burnside
   isn't failing for lack of demand. Frame as: momentum climbs north through midtown and stops
   at the seam (a High Crash Corridor, 4 deaths/decade, now being repaved as-is). The stadium
   cluster (15th–20th) is the far shore. This goes on the site (if at all) as one sourced
   contrast paragraph/map layer in the Evidence section — NOT as a revived status board.
2. **Playbook.** All four had public capital or public land. Prosper Portland has now financed
   two food halls within a 12-minute walk of the ex-Chipotle block. "Aim the same instrument at
   the stadium cluster" is a concrete, precedented ask (sharpens Plan moves 2/3/4 and the
   Officials ask about Vacant Spaces grants).
3. **Recruiting list.** Flock/JBPM orbit = Prosper relationships; MBG = cart-pod operators who
   activate cheap space; Darcelle plaza = Parks programming staff. All candidates for the
   coalition's "fill the storefronts" and match-day programming work.
4. **The hinge is the Park Blocks.** Darcelle plaza sits on the Park Ave axis; North Park
   Blocks are directly across Burnside. The pool's most natural route north crosses Burnside at
   the Park Blocks — useful for the "light the string of pearls" move and for any crossing-safety
   ask in the repave fight.
5. **Hashagen/letter-coalition line:** "The demand is already three blocks south of the street —
   Midtown Beer Garden, Flock, Darcelle plaza, and the public market this fall. The corridor's
   job is to give it a reason to cross Burnside."
