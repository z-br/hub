## Burnside Triangle heritage angle (eastern corridor)

The blocks around W Burnside/SW Harvey Milk St (10th–13th) were the **Burnside Triangle** — Portland's historic gay district from the 1960s-70s on (the Eagle, CC Slaughters, Scandals, Silverado, Boxxes, Red Cap Garage, Club Portland). Gentrification hollowed it out by ~2017 ("the Pink Triangle isn't very pink anymore" — Oregonian). The city has recently begun honoring this heritage: Darcelle XV Plaza (opened 6/2026), Harvey Milk St renaming, Crystal Hotel NRHP listing (2024, partly for LGBTQ significance), PBOT Pride + Darcelle XV street plazas. **Revival lever:** a formal LGBTQ cultural district designation (Castro/Northalsted model) would give the eastern corridor identity, programming, and grant eligibility that generic streetscape asks lack — pairs with the stadium anchor at the western end.
