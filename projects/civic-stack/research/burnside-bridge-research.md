# Burnside Bridge + W Burnside Corridor — Verified Research (July 18, 2026)

Deep-research pass: 19 sources fetched, 88 claims extracted, 25 adversarially verified (3 refuted). High-confidence findings only.

## The headline: the closure is postponed indefinitely
- **Oct 20, 2025:** Multnomah County abandoned the planned 2028 construction start for the Earthquake Ready Burnside Bridge (EQRB). No new date set. Cause: federal funding uncertainty — only ~$7M of a needed ~$740M federal share secured — and cost growth to **$1.6–1.8B**. County hub page (checked July 2, 2026) lists no start date, closure dates, or detour plans.
- **Design continues:** design phase extended; 60% design milestone targeted for summer 2026 so the project stays "construction-ready" when funding lands. (No public confirmation the milestone was actually hit — re-anchor task pending.)
- **Schedule history:** 2020 plan said construction 2024, 4–5 yrs → late 2023: closure slated 2027 (potential reopen 2031) → Mar 2025: delayed to 2028 → Oct 2025: indefinite. All prior dates superseded.
- **Full closure stands:** adopted approach is full bridge closure with detours to neighboring bridges — the temporary bridge was rejected (~+1.5–2 yrs, ~$90M). This creates the multi-year "bridgeless interval." Detailed detour/transit-reroute plans have NOT been published.

**Implication:** the PSU plan's 2028–2033 window is superseded; the closure comes *when funded*. Reframe: the delay = organizing/prep time; federal funding advocacy is itself a corridor issue (and a gift to offer the County).

## Transit facts (verified)
- Eastbound transit priority on W Burnside benefits TriMet **lines 12, 19, 20**:
  - Burnside Bridge bus-only lane (W 2nd/3rd → E MLK): **built 2019** (Rose Lane).
  - BAT lane extension **3rd → 8th Ave** (CCIM/Rose Lane, with Old Town Community Association): schedule was "under development"; on-ground status as of 7/2026 unconfirmed.
  - BAT lane **Broadway → W 2nd**: funded, design/construction phase (per PBOT, current as of Mar 2026).
- Refuted (1-2): the claim that Line 20 alone is "the" Burnside corridor line — 12 and 19 also cross the bridge.

## Bridgeless to Better Burnside (verified)
- **June 2025 PSU MURP Workshop report**; authors Azierski, Bill, Gasper, Gill, Morrison, Rojas; **client: Better Block PDX**.
- Two phases: temporary interventions during the closure → permanent improvements after reopening. Scope: lower West Burnside.
- Concepts: Park Blocks (most ambitious) — bollard-buffered shared bike/bus lane each direction, parklets, food carts, pedestrian-scale lighting, raised crosswalks; Third Ave — right-turn lane at Dante's → public parklet, median expansion + street mural; "Burnside Sunday Parkways" cultural programming.
- Pipeline: Spring 2024 data/engagement → Fall 2024 design studio → Winter/Spring 2025 civil-engineering capstone (engineering plans + permitting materials). Whether permitting-ready materials were completed is UNCONFIRMED (claim refuted 1-2). Proposals only — no verified funding, permits, or agency endorsement yet.
- **Champions:** Xavier Stickler (chair, Downtown NA Land Use & Transportation Cmte), Sean Sweat (Pearl District NA), Mary-Rain O'Meara (Old Town Community Association board) — joint Better Block PSU applicants; **Ryan Hashagen** leads Better Block PDX (also owns Icicle Tricycles in Old Town; PSU adjunct; wrote the 3/2025 BikePortland Better Burnside article).
