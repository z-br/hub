# The Bridgehead Rain Pavilion — concept + the McCall window

## The concept (GZ, 7/21/26)
Mass-timber **covered playground + beer garden + fire pits** on Waterfront Park's quiet block
**between Ankeny and Ash, just south of Saturday Market** — the stretch that isn't part of the
main festival zone. Designed to flourish in summer AND rain:
- **Operable louvered roof** — opens fully in good weather, closes to a warm dry room in bad.
- **Rain as the attraction**: clear walls with rain sheeting through/over them, backlit; fires
  going; kids dry under timber. Counter-programming for a rain city — the only park space in
  Portland that gets *better* in November.
- Mass timber as identity: Oregon's material, on Oregon's front porch.

## Why this is corridor work (not waterfront scope creep)
- **East anchor / bookends**: Providence Park anchors W Burnside's west end; the bridgehead
  anchors the east. The crossings-trail terminus ("The river & the bridge," W 2nd & Ankeny)
  points straight at this block.
- **Saturday Market** delivers weekend crowds to its edge already; the pavilion keeps them
  past 5pm and through winter.
- **Bridge-closure logic**: when EQRB construction finally comes, the bridgehead is a dead end
  unless it's a destination — this is "pilot now, scale in the closure" for the east end.
- Waterfront-WIDE ambitions stay parked (no owner); this single block is the corridor's edge.

## THE CLOCK: McCall design competition is live NOW (verified 7/21/26)
- PP&R launched the **Gov. Tom McCall Waterfront Park design competition July 8, 2026**.
- **Two-stage**: Stage 1 = team qualifications, **deadline July 30, 2026**; ~shortlist gets
  $50K each for Stage 2 concepts (Stage 2 from ~mid-September); **winner spring 2027**.
- Managed by **Malcolm Reading Consultants**; **Interplay** (Portland social-impact
  consultancy) runs community engagement; funded by a **$750K Metro grant**.
- GZ's play is NOT a Stage 1 team entry (that's for architect-led teams): it's (a) the
  **Interplay engagement channel** — get the rain-forward east-anchor program into the brief
  and in front of shortlisted teams; (b) a champions-blessed concept one-pager the coalition
  can submit collectively; (c) Rose Light coverage of the competition itself.

## Verified precedents & levers
- **Director Park** (SW 9th & Yamhill): OLIN/ZGF plaza with a **v-shaped glass canopy** that
  channels rain into a fall toward the fountain — the mini proof that covered + rain-featured
  public space works here. (Earlier "outdoor fireplace" claim NOT verified — dropped until
  someone checks on foot.)
- **PDX airport mass-timber roof** (2024): the beloved hometown proof of the material; the
  mass-timber industry (Oregon Mass Timber Coalition, TallWood Design Institute — verify
  current programs) actively seeks civic showcases → a funding/advocacy constituency.
- **Parks vending + beer gardens** (see parks-vending-precedents.md): PP&R mobile-vending
  program live since Oct 2025 (parking areas, no alcohol); beer gardens legal today as
  events (special-use + OLCC temporary); Parks-on-Tap rotating model is ask #2; concessions-
  as-revenue (NYC/Bryant Park) is the conservancy-funding logic.
- **Seattle lesson #1** (seattle-portland-downtown-lessons.md): the park needs an institution
  — Friends of Waterfront Park + LID funding. A revenue anchor (beer garden/concessions) is
  what makes a Portland conservancy fundable; competition coverage says a nonprofit/funding
  model is part of the conversation.
