## 1. Neighborhood paper editors

Send individually (never CC the group). Each email is short, honest, and leads
with what's in it for them: readers.

**Recipients & where to find them:**
- NW Examiner — nwexaminer.com (contact/masthead page)
- Southeast Examiner — southeastexaminer.com
- The Bee (Sellwood) — thebeenews.com
- St. Johns Review — stjohnsreview.com

**Subject:** Your paper is featured on The Rose Light (free Portland news hub)

> Hi [Editor name],
>
> I'm a Portland reader and I built The Rose Light (theroselight.org) — a free
> aggregator that puts every local Portland story on one page: OPB, the
> Mercury, KGW… and the neighborhood papers, including yours.
>
> Every [NW Examiner] headline on the site links straight to your website —
> the goal is to send readers *to* local journalism, not replace it. There are
> no ads and no paywall; it's a personal project to make Portland news easier
> to find.
>
> Two things:
> 1. If you'd ever like your feed removed or handled differently, one email
>    and it's done.
> 2. If you think your readers would find the site useful, I'd be honored by
>    a mention — the weekly roundup lives at theroselight.org/this-week.
>
> Thanks for covering [the neighborhood] — the site exists because work like
> yours deserves more eyes.
>
> [Your name]
> [your email]

**Personalization per paper (replace the bracketed bits):**
- NW Examiner → "covering Northwest Portland"
- Southeast Examiner → "covering inner Southeast"
- The Bee → "covering Sellwood-Moreland and Westmoreland"
- St. Johns Review → "covering St. Johns and North Portland"

## 2. r/Portland post

**Read first:** r/Portland mods remove self-promo fast. Before posting,
message the mods: "I built a free, no-ads Portland news aggregator — OK to
share?" A yes turns the post bulletproof. Best timing: weekday morning.

**Title options (pick one):**
- I got tired of paywalls and national noise, so I built a free page with every Portland story
- I made a free site that pulls all of Portland's local news (OPB, Mercury, neighborhood papers) onto one page

**Body:**

> Like a lot of you I've watched local news get harder to follow — the
> Oregonian's paywalled, stuff is scattered across a dozen sites, and every
> feed I open is 80% national politics.
>
> So I built **The Rose Light** (theroselight.org): every Portland story from
> 22 local sources on one page. OPB, the Mercury, KGW, BikePortland, Eater
> PDX, Oregon Capital Chronicle — plus the neighborhood papers most people
> don't know have websites (NW Examiner, SE Examiner, The Bee, St. Johns
> Review).
>
> The rules I built it around:
> - **Local only.** National/world stories get filtered out of the main feed
>   (they live in a separate corner if you want them).
> - **Free, no ads, no login.** Headlines link straight to the original
>   outlet — the point is sending readers to local journalism.
> - There's a weekly roundup at /this-week and an optional Sunday email, but
>   you can just use the homepage and never sign up for anything.
>
> It's a personal project and I'd genuinely love feedback — missing sources,
> broken stuff, whatever. What local outlets should I add?

**After posting:** stay in the comments for the first 2 hours. Answer
everything, take every source suggestion seriously (each commenter becomes an
evangelist), and don't get defensive about the AI curation if it comes up —
"the AI just scores how Portland-relevant a story is; everything links to the
original reporting" is the honest answer.
