## Global style block (append to EVERY prompt)
```
Pacific Northwest blue-hour evening, light rain, wet pavement reflections,
warm amber and burnt-orange (#eb6834) accent lighting against cool dusk,
people with umbrellas mid-stride (diverse, candid, not posed), photorealistic
concept-illustration quality, architectural visualization style, 3:2 aspect,
no text or signage except where specified, consistent across a series
```

## Photo-edit instruction template (use with your own site photo)
```
Keep this exact street scene, camera angle, buildings, and weather. Add: [INTERVENTION].
Match the existing light direction; make added elements photoreal and consistent
with the scene's scale. Do not alter or remove existing buildings or signs.
```
